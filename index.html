About Us

Trading Steel in Faridabad Since 2012

Hisar Stainless Industries has been supplying steel pipes, sheets, angles, patti and bullester to fabricators, contractors and retail customers from Sector 62-64 Chowk for over a decade.

Alongside raw material, we stock the small items every job site needs — cutting wheels, flap discs, buff and kanche — so you're not running to three shops for one project. And when the job calls for a finished gate or railing, our fabricator network turns your requirement into steel.
